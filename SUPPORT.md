## Русский

AlphaEmu - бесплатный (для некоммерческого использования) эмулятор
AlphaServer ES40. Его пишет один человек в свободное время. OpenVMS Alpha
V8.3 на нём уже работает: до четырёх CPU, сеть, VMScluster, DECwindows,
JIT на x86-64 и aarch64. Но работы впереди много, и ваша помощь ускорит её.

### На что пойдут деньги

- **Новые устройства.** Адаптер SCSI 53C810 и лента (BACKUP на ленту),
  новые диски и сетевые адаптеры, модели других AlphaServer.
- **Больше систем.** Проверка других версий OpenVMS Alpha.
- **Скорость.** Развитие JIT, чтобы гость работал быстрее настоящей машины.
- **Проверки.** Долгие соак-тесты на разных хостах.
- **Документация.** Руководство на русском, больше примеров.
- **Железо для стенда.** Диски и машины для тестов.

## English

AlphaEmu is a free (for non-commercial use) emulator of the AlphaServer
ES40. One person writes it in their spare time. OpenVMS Alpha V8.3 already
runs on it: up to four CPUs, network, VMScluster, DECwindows, JIT on x86-64
and aarch64. But there is still a lot of work to do, and your help makes it
go faster.

### What the money is for

- **New devices.** The 53C810 SCSI adapter and tape (BACKUP to tape), new
  disks and network cards, models of other AlphaServers.
- **More systems.** Testing other OpenVMS Alpha versions.
- **Speed.** More work on the JIT, so the guest runs faster than real
  hardware.
- **Testing.** Long soak tests on different hosts.
- **Documentation.** More examples, a guide in Russian.
- **Test hardware.** Disks and machines for the test bench.

### How to help

| Way | Where |
|---|---|
| USDT, USDC, BNB - **BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)** network | `0x0f73bDeDF7d7699Ec235FCEc2466C8F0bb0e3016` |

Send **only on the BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)** network. A transfer on another
network (TRON, Bitcoin, etc.) to this address may be lost.

Any amount helps. A donation is a gift to the project: it gives no
commercial licence and does not change the terms of `EULA.txt`.

Money is not the only way to help:

- **Report a bug**, or tell us what works for you: the Issues of this
  repository (https://codeberg.org/SysManOne/yaAlphaEmu).
- **Tell other people** who may need the emulator.
- **Help with testing** on your own OpenVMS or your own hardware.

### For companies

Do you need a commercial licence, changes for your own tasks, help to move
a system off a real Alpha, or support with a fixed response time? Write to
zator@yandex.ru.

### Please note

OpenVMS, its media and its licences (PAKs) do not come with the emulator.
You must get them from the rights holder. The emulator does not need the DEC
firmware.
